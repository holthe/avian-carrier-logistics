Avian Carrier Logistics is a division of Best Effort Industries, operating under RFC 1149 (Standard for the transmission of IP datagrams on avian carriers), RFC 2549 (QoS extensions), and RFC 6214 (IPv6 adaptation). These documents are real and may be consulted. The company is not, and may not.

Carriage is subject to weather, hawks, bread, magnetic anomalies, and the carrier's personal judgment, which is final. Multipath routing occurs when the flock scatters. Packets are non-refundable once released, as we cannot get them back either.
